How it works

Blue is the position now. Each row down is one more move ahead. Faded branches are skipped.

Board in memoryA 128-slot array (the "0x88" layout). One quick bit test tells the program when a move runs off the board.
Every legal moveCastling, en passant and promotion included. Moves that leave the king in check are dropped. It matches the standard test counts, such as 197,281 positions after two moves each.
Scoring a positionPiece values and good squares, blended smoothly from middlegame to endgame. It also scores king safety, passed, doubled and isolated pawns, how freely each piece can move, and rooks on open files.
Looking aheadMinimax with alpha-beta pruning, one level deeper at a time until the clock runs out. A memory of positions it has already seen (a transposition table) and smart shortcuts let it look about 8 moves ahead in a couple of seconds.
Opening bookAbout 35 main-line openings, so the first moves are sound and instant.
Game reviewEach position is analysed, and moves are rated by how much they lower your chance to win: inaccuracy, mistake or blunder.
Built from scratchPlain JavaScript, no chess libraries. The thinking runs in background workers so the board never freezes.
