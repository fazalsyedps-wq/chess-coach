How it works

Blue is the position now. Each row down is one more move ahead. Faded branches are skipped.

Board in memoryA 128-slot array (the "0x88" layout). One quick bit test tells the program when a move runs off the board.
Every legal moveCastling, en passant and promotion included. Moves that leave the king in check are dropped. It matches the standard test counts, such as 197,281 positions after two moves each.
Scoring a positionPawn 1, knight 3.2, bishop 3.3, rook 5, queen 9, plus small bonuses for good squares, like a knight in the centre.
Looking aheadMinimax with alpha-beta pruning. It searches one level deeper at a time until the clock runs out, then follows captures to the end so it never stops mid-trade.
Built from scratchPlain JavaScript, no chess libraries. The search runs in a background worker so the board never freezes.
